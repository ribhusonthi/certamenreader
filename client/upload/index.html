Expected Question Format

The parser expects certamen questions formatted like this:

Round One

1. What verb means "to give" in Latin?
DO - GIVE
B1: What derivative of do means "to unintentionally reveal"?
BETRAY
B2: What derivative of do refers to property brought by a bride?
DOWRY

2. What woman fled from a lion at Ninus's tomb?
THISBE
B1: Blood from Pyramus stains the fruit of what tree?
MULBERRY
B2: Pyramus and Thisbe lived during the reign of what ruler?
SEMIRAMIS

Questions should be numbered (1., 2., etc. or TU:). Answers appear on their own line in ALL CAPS after the question. Bonuses are marked with B1:, B2:, etc. Round headers are automatically detected.
