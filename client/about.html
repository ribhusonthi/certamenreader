About

About Certamen Reader

A text-based Certamen packet reader created by Ribhu Sonthi, adapted from Geoffrey Wu's QB Reader.

Packets are collected from certamenlibrary.org and parsed into JSON files using a Python-based Certamen packet parser.

Why Certamen Reader?

  • Uses the latest set of questions. Packets are uploaded to the website as soon as they are released.
  • Can filter questions by tossup, difficulty, and packet

Account Benefits

Making a free account with a verified email unlocks:

  • Tracking your personal tossup and bonus stats.
  • Contributing to community-wide per-question stats.
  • Viewing per-question stats directly in the Database page.
  • Access to practice tests.
