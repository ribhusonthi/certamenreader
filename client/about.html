A text-based Certamen packet reader created by Ribhu Sonthi,

adapted from Geoffrey Wu's QB Reader.

Packets are collected from certamenlibrary.org and parsed into JSON files using a Python-based Certamen packet parser.

Why Certamen Reader?

  • Uses the latest set of questions. Packets are uploaded to the website as soon as they are released.
  • Can filter questions by tossup, difficulty, and packet

What does making an account do?

Making an account with a verified email gives you the following features:

  • Lets you track your tossup and bonus stats.
  • Contributes to community-wide per-question stats.
  • Allows you to view per-question stats in the database page.
  • Lets you access practice tests

Does it cost money to create an account?

No.

Can I get a copy of all the questions?
