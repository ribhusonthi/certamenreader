About Certamen Reader

A text-based Certamen packet reader created by Ribhu Sonthi, adapted from Geoffrey Wu's QB Reader. Packets are collected from certamenlibrary.org and parsed into JSON using a Python-based Certamen packet parser.

Why Certamen Reader?

  • Uses the latest set of questions — packets are uploaded as soon as they are released.
  • Filter questions by tossup, difficulty, and packet.
  • Buzz anywhere in a tossup, type your answer, and track your celerity against the power mark.
  • Keyboard-first, NJCL scoring by default — designed to feel like a moderator across the table.

Account benefits

Making a free account with a verified email unlocks:

  • Tracking your personal tossup and bonus stats.
  • Contributing to community-wide per-question stats.
  • Viewing per-question stats directly on the Database page.
  • Access to practice tests.
